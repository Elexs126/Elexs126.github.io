“我并不将全尺寸人形机器人视为机械工程的终点，而是将其视为通用认知模型与真实物理定律发生自然交互的理想载体。”
“I view full-size humanoid robots not as the endpoint of mechanical engineering, but as the primary physical vessel for a generalized cognitive model to naturally interact with physical laws.”

我的研究兴趣集中于智能系统的学习、表征与决策机制，尤其关注能够在真实物理环境中感知并行动的智能体。我希望理解，智能体如何通过与环境的持续交互形成对世界的认识，并将经验转化为可泛化的推理与行动能力。具身智能为探索这些问题提供了重要场景，人形机器人则是其中承载和检验认知架构的核心载体。

在研究取向上，我关注能够解释系统行为、揭示现有方法局限，并推动架构改进的理论与机制。我重视问题的抽象层次：有效的解释需要保留决定行为差异的关键结构，并明确数学表述、学习算法与系统能力之间的联系。这也意味着，理论的价值既体现在形式上的清晰与一致，也体现在它能否带来新的预测、设计原则和可检验的改进。

我倾向于围绕关键问题提出架构假设，通过必要的理论分析与系统实验检验其成立条件和适用边界。长期而言，我希望探索支撑智能体持续学习、适应环境与自主行动的通用机制，并在理论理解与智能系统设计之间建立更紧密的联系。

My research focuses on the learning, representation, and decision-making mechanisms of intelligent systems, with a particular emphasis on agents that perceive and act in physical environments. I seek to understand how agents construct representations through continuous environmental interaction and translate experience into generalizable reasoning and action capabilities. Embodied AI provides an essential foundation for exploring these questions, with humanoid robots serving as a primary testbed for evaluating cognitive architectures.

In terms of research approach, I prioritize theories and mechanisms that explain system behavior, reveal methodological limitations, and drive architectural improvements. I place great value on the level of abstraction: effective explanations must retain critical structures governing behavioral divergence while formalizing the connections between mathematical principles, learning algorithms, and system capabilities—delivering formal clarity alongside verifiable predictions.

I aim to formulate architectural hypotheses around core challenges, validating their operating assumptions and boundaries through theoretical analysis and systematic experimentation. Long term, I aspire to explore universal mechanisms supporting lifelong learning and autonomous adaptation, bridging theoretical insight and intelligent system design.

人形智能体动态行走策略的纯粹机制复现与剥离实验

First-Principles Reproduction of Humanoid Locomotion via DRL

机制复现 Mechanism Analysis

出于对底层强化学习动力学耦合机制的探究，使用 PyTorch 从零构建 PPO 训练管线。实验中刻意移除了繁杂而缺乏理论解释的工程试错（Ad-hoc Reward Shaping Tricks），独立验证了核心奖励结构在物理仿真中对步态对称性与能量效率的边界影响。

Driven by curiosity about the underlying coupling between RL optimization and rigid-body dynamics, I built the PPO pipeline from scratch in PyTorch. By deliberately stripping away ad-hoc reward-shaping tricks, this work isolates and verifies the theoretical impact of core objective structures on gait symmetry and energetic efficiency.

稀疏奖励环境下的跨序列注意力泛化探索

Cross-Sequence Attention for Policy Generalization in Continuous Control

架构探索 Architectural Probe

尝试将类 Transformer 的跨序列注意力机制引入在线强化学习范式中，用于捕捉长视距物理交互中的隐含动力学特征，并重点考察与记录了策略模型在未见物理测试环境中的零样本泛化表现。

An experimental probe into integrating Transformer-style cross-sequence attention into on-policy RL paradigms—capturing implicit dynamical features over long horizons and evaluating zero-shot policy generalization across unseen physical environments.
