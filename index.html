Undergraduate Researcher @ Xi'an University of Architecture and Technology (XAUAT).
Focusing on Whole-Body Control and World Models for Full-Size Humanoid Robots.

本科生 @ 西安建筑科技大学 (XAUAT)。
致力于全尺寸人形机器人的全身控制 (Whole-Body Control) 与世界模型 (World Models) 研究。

我的研究兴趣集中于智能系统的学习、表征与决策机制，尤其关注能够在真实物理环境中感知并行动的智能体。我希望理解，智能体如何通过与环境的持续交互形成对世界的认识，并将经验转化为可泛化的推理与行动能力。具身智能为探索这些问题提供了重要场景，人形机器人则是其中承载和检验认知架构的核心物理载体。
研究取向 · 抽象层次 01

理论与机制导向

关注能够解释系统行为、揭示现有方法局限并推动架构改进的理论与机制。有效的解释需要保留决定行为差异的关键结构，并在数学表述、学习算法与系统能力之间建立明确映射。理论的价值既体现在形式上的清晰与一致，更体现在它能否带来新的预测、设计原则和可检验的改进。

研究方法 · 演进愿景 02

架构假设与系统闭环

倾向于围绕关键问题提出架构假设，通过必要的理论分析与系统实验检验其成立条件和适用边界。长期而言，探索支撑智能体持续学习、适应环境与自主行动的通用机制，在理论理解与智能系统工程设计之间建立更紧密的联系。

My research focuses on the learning, representation, and decision-making mechanisms of intelligent systems, with a particular emphasis on agents that perceive and act in physical environments. I seek to understand how agents construct world models through continuous interaction and translate experience into generalizable reasoning and action capabilities—where Embodied AI serves as the foundational scenario and humanoid robots as the primary physical vessel for evaluating cognitive architectures.
Perspective · Abstraction 01

Theory & Mechanism-Driven

I prioritize theories that explain system behaviors, reveal the limitations of current methodologies, and drive principled architectural improvements. Effective explanations must preserve critical structures governing behavioral divergence while formalizing the bridge between mathematical formulations, learning algorithms, and system capabilities—delivering formal clarity alongside verifiable predictions.

Methodology · Vision 02

Hypothesis & Universal Adaptation

I formulate architectural hypotheses around core challenges, probing their validity conditions and operational bounds through theoretical analysis and systematic experimentation. Long term, I aim to uncover universal mechanisms supporting lifelong learning and autonomous adaptation, bridging the gap between theoretical insight and embodied system design.
